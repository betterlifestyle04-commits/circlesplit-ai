Open to everyone

1 · Explore the public experience

  1. Open the public CircleSplit demo and select Event planner.
  2. Choose Friday Community Volleyball: $220 for 20 friends.
  3. Verify equal splitting yields $11.00 each and weighted splitting adjusts allocations without losing cents.
  4. Create a $10 event with three fictional participants and confirm the exact shares are $3.34, $3.33 and $3.33, adding up to $10.00.
  5. Open Contributions, approve a share and select Simulate; it must be labelled SIMULATED and must not charge anyone.
  6. Open public demo, select Resilience lab and select the $220 / 20-person example. With a $12 share cap, 2 hypothetical no-shows require 1 fictional replacement attendee; 5 no-shows require 4. This runs entirely in your browser with no payment or login.

Reset demo restores local fictional data. This experience never contacts Gemini or PayPal.

2 · Run the AI copilot

  1. Sign into the protected workspace with judge credentials supplied privately in Devpost.
  2. Open AI assistant and paste the sample note.
  3. Click Analyze expenses. A real Gemini response should extract $180, $25 and $30, totalling $235, and provide a rationale.
  4. Verify that every proposal remains editable and does not change an event until it is explicitly approved.

Private participant invitation

3 · Inspect informed share consent

  1. In the protected organizer workspace, create an event with expenses and participants. Generate a private invitation link for one fictional participant.
  2. Open the private link. Confirm it shows only that participant's share, total estimated budget, participant count, equal/weighted split and cost categories—not the other participants' names or shares.
  3. Approve the displayed amount. This records fictional consent only; it does not initiate a PayPal transaction.
  4. Before creating any checkout, test the withdrawal button. Withdrawal blocks a new order until a fresh participant-link approval.

Never publish invitation bearer links. They are not identity proof; the organizer and judge can use fictional data only. After a checkout starts, withdrawal cannot cancel or refund it.

Judge: inspect · Organizer: execute

4 · Review Sandbox capture evidence

  1. With the separately supplied judge login, open the protected workspace, navigate to Activity, and inspect any local Sandbox ledger entries and read-only audits. VERIFIED CAPTURE means a checked Sandbox capture; ORDER CREATED is not paid, and SIMULATED is not a provider payment.
  2. Do not attempt PayPal test, Check PayPal, or capture as a judge. Those controls require organizer credentials and are intentionally disabled or rejected for the judge role.
  3. Review the public under-three-minute YouTube demonstration of the organizer-run flow: an $11 fictional contribution, a PayPal Sandbox personal buyer approving on a trusted Sandbox page, followed by a verified capture in Activity. Never use a real card or share buyer credentials.
  4. If the current ledger contains no captures, that does not disprove prior recorded testing: Render Free's temporary SQLite data can reset on redeploy. Use the authentic recorded video and GitHub implementation/tests for evidence, rather than repeating an uncertain payment.
  5. Only an authorized organizer may perform a new Sandbox checkout or reconcile an existing order. Never repeat a pending creation or capture just because a response timed out.

This judge flow is read-only for provider activity. Gemini and fictional planning are testable with judge credentials if the service is configured and available; provider-key presence and healthy deployment do not independently prove a completed payment.
