PayPal sandbox

A payment you can trace

The backend includes sandbox Orders v2 creation, capture checks, idempotency and webhook signature verification.

  1. Participant approves an exact-cent share.
  2. Organizer creates a sandbox PayPal order.
  3. Sandbox buyer approves payment.
  4. Server verifies capture amount and status.
  5. Verified contribution appears in the ledger.
Public payments are disabled to prevent unauthorized orders and API abuse. One live Gemini response and one $11.00 PayPal SANDBOX capture were documented in the protected workspace on November 2–3, 2026. This is not an independent audit or a guarantee of ongoing availability.
